Wellness yoga retreats for individuals

Step into a beautiful morning of calm, connection, and renewal with our immersive Summer Yoga Retreat experience. Running from 9:00am – 2:30pm, this thoughtfully designed retreat is created to nourish both body and mind.

Your journey begins with a soothing herbal tea, followed by an introductory asana series and a deeper Hatha Yoga practice. You’ll explore mindful alignment and adjustments, alongside calming pranayama (yogic breathwork) to restore balance and inner peace.

Enjoy a lovingly prepared homemade satvik Indian breakfast and a wholesome traditional Indian lunch, philosophy and discussion, and end the day with a deeply restorative sound bath and Yoga Nidra - leaving you grounded, refreshed, and uplifted.
