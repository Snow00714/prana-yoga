Wellness Yoga Retreats for Individuals

This immersive retreat morning is designed for calm, connection, and renewal. You begin with herbal tea, then gentle asana and deeper Hatha Yoga, alignment, pranayama, and guided rest practices.

The day also includes nourishing homemade satvik breakfast and traditional Indian lunch, philosophy learning, and restorative sound bath or Yoga Nidra to leave you grounded and refreshed.
