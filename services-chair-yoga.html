Chair Yoga for Care Homes

Chair Yoga sessions are gentle, accessible, and uplifting, supporting physical and emotional wellbeing through seated movement, mindful stretching, and calming breathwork.

Sessions are delivered with patience and compassion, with experience in supporting disabled individuals and people living with dementia.
