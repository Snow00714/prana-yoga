# prana-yoga

## About me
Hi, I’m a certified yoga instructor trained in the sacred city of Rishikesh — known around the world as the Yoga Capital of the World.

My journey into yoga began with a deep passion for movement, mindfulness, and connection. Training in India allowed me to immerse myself in the authentic roots of yoga, learning ancient practices that nurture both body and mind.

Today, I’m proud to bring that traditional wisdom, calming energy, and uplifting spirit directly into our local community. My classes are designed to help you feel stronger, more balanced, and more connected — whether you’re stepping onto the mat for the very first time or deepening an existing practice.

Expect a welcoming space filled with positive energy, mindful movement, and moments of calm, joy, and self-discovery. ✨

Yoga is more than exercise — it’s a journey back to yourself.

## Services
### Yoga Classes
Small, welcoming classes designed with you in mind. By keeping class sizes intimate, I’m able to offer personalised guidance, individual attention, and supportive adjustments throughout your practice.

Whether you’re a complete beginner or an experienced yogi, you’ll feel encouraged, comfortable, and connected every step of the way. My classes combine mindful movement, breathwork, strength, relaxation, and positive energy to help you leave feeling refreshed, balanced, and uplifted.

### Wellness Yoga Retreats for individuals 
Step into a beautiful morning of calm, connection, and renewal with our immersive Summer Yoga Retreat experience. Running from 9:00am – 2:30pm, this thoughtfully designed retreat is created to nourish both body and mind.

Your journey begins with a soothing herbal tea to gently awaken the senses, followed by an introductory asana series and a deeper Hatha Yoga practice. You’ll explore mindful alignment and adjustments to support and strengthen your practice, alongside calming pranayama (breathwork) to restore balance and inner peace.

Midway through the retreat, enjoy a lovingly prepared homemade satvik Indian breakfast, followed later by a wholesome traditional Indian lunch — both designed to energise, nourish, and comfort.

You’ll also deepen your understanding of yoga philosophy through guided discussion and learning, before ending the day with a deeply restorative sound bath and Yoga Nidra session. Herbal tea, biscuits, and a special souvenir gift complete the experience, leaving you grounded, refreshed, and uplifted. ✨

### Wellness Yoga Retreats for Staff/Corporate days 
In today’s fast-paced working world, mental wellbeing and stress relief are more important than ever. Long hours at desks, constant screen time, and back-to-back meetings can leave teams feeling exhausted, overwhelmed, and disconnected.

My Corporate Wellness Retreats are designed to help staff recharge, refocus, and reconnect — creating happier, healthier workplaces with improved wellbeing, productivity, morale, and reduced absenteeism.

A typical programme runs from 9:00am – 2:30pm and includes:

Herbal tea welcome
Gentle mindful stretching and flowing yoga practice
Alignment-focused movement and posture guidance
Calming pranayama (breathwork)
Homemade satvik Indian breakfast
Traditional homemade Indian lunch
Restorative sound bath or Yoga Nidra session
Herbal tea and light refreshments

Each retreat can be fully tailored to suit your organisation’s needs, whether you’re looking for yoga, meditation, breathwork, mindfulness, or sound healing experiences. Sessions can take place at your office or in a local venue organised for your team.

Together, we can create a wellness experience that supports both your staff and your workplace culture. ✨

### Chair Yoga for Care Homes
Gentle, accessible, and uplifting movement for residents of all abilities.

My Chair Yoga sessions are designed to support both the physical and emotional wellbeing of care home residents through gentle yoga-inspired movement, mindful stretching, and calming yogic breathwork.

Regular practice can help improve mobility, circulation, posture, flexibility, relaxation, and overall wellbeing — while also creating moments of connection, joy, and calm.

I also have experience supporting disabled individuals and those living with dementia. For complete peace of mind, I am fully insured, hold an Enhanced DBS check, and am recognised as a Dementia Friend.

Every session is delivered with warmth, patience, compassion, and respect for each individual’s needs and abilities. ✨
