Yoga Nidra, Meditation and Sound Bath Workshops

These workshops are designed to help you slow down, reset, and reconnect through guided Yoga Nidra, meditation, breathwork, mindfulness, and restorative sound experiences.

Planned as weekly sessions over a set course, they create a nurturing space for stress relief, deeper rest, emotional balance, and inner calm.
