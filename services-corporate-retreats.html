Wellness yoga retreats for staff / corporate days

In today’s fast-paced working world, mental wellbeing and stress relief matter more than ever. Corporate Wellness Retreats help teams recharge, refocus, and reconnect — supporting morale, productivity, and a healthier workplace culture.

A typical programme runs from 9:00am – 2:30pm and can include herbal tea welcome, gentle mindful stretching and flowing yoga, alignment-focused movement, pranayama, nourishing satvik breakfast and traditional Indian lunch, and a restorative sound bath or Yoga Nidra session.

Each retreat can be tailored to your organisation — yoga, meditation, breathwork, mindfulness, or sound healing — at your office or a local venue. Together we can create a wellness experience that supports your staff and your culture.
