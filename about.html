About me

Hi, I’m a certified yoga instructor trained in the sacred city of Rishikesh — known around the world as the Yoga Capital of the World.

My journey into yoga began with a deep passion for movement, mindfulness, and connection. Training in India allowed me to immerse myself in the authentic roots of yoga, learning ancient practices that nurture both body and mind.

Today, I’m proud to bring that traditional wisdom, calming energy, and uplifting spirit directly into our local community. My classes are designed to help you feel stronger, more balanced, and more connected — whether you’re stepping onto the mat for the very first time or deepening an existing practice.

Expect a welcoming space filled with positive energy, mindful movement, and moments of calm, joy, and self-discovery. Yoga is more than exercise — it’s a journey back to yourself.
